About

Gene Dai is a product builder and Co-Founder & CPO of Metix AI, an AI hiring platform that reached #1 on Product Hunt. He previously held senior product leadership roles at BOSS Zhipin (BZ/2076.HK), China's leading mobile recruiting platform, Liepin (6100.HK), Zuoyebang, and Zhidemai Tech (300785.SZ), spanning recruiting, education, and e-commerce. He writes on AI and HRTech across Medium, Substack, and his personal blog, and maintains 160+ open-source repositories on GitHub.

  • AI Hiring — Building Metix: define a role and meet interview-ready candidates within 24 hours.
  • Growth & Platforms — User growth, community, and platform products for consumer apps in recruiting, education, and e-commerce.
  • Domain Expertise — Built products for all three sides of hiring (candidates, employers, and headhunters) at Kanzhun, BOSS Zhipin, and Liepin.
  • Builder & Writer — Writing on AI, HRTech, and the people shaping the industry.
